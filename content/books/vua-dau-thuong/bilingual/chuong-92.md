<p class="para-en">He must’ve seen her amusement on her face because he nodded, his gaze distant. “All right. You had your chance to come clean. I’ll let it go for now. We’ll take it up again—next time we talk.”</p>

<p class="para-en">She thought they’d talk again tomorrow morning, same as always. But next time came sooner than that.</p>
